Root-Cause Explaination Related Knowledge Base
By tracing the call frequency of log_buffer_flush_to_disk() function, we
observe that this function is frequently triggered, indicating that the redo
log buffer capacity is insufficient to hold the log data generated by the
current workload. When the redo log buffer overflows, the system is
forced to frequently flush the log buffer contents to disk, causing a sharp
increase in disk I/O operations, which then becomes the main bottleneck
